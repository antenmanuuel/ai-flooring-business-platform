# Caring for carpet

## Regular care
Vacuum high-traffic areas at least twice a week and the rest of the room weekly. Blot spills straight away with a clean white cloth; don't rub, which spreads the stain and damages the fibers.

## Deep cleaning
Have carpet deep-cleaned (hot water extraction) about every 12 to 18 months, more often with pets or children. Many carpet warranties expect regular professional cleaning.

## Where carpet works best
Carpet is soft, warm and quiet, which suits bedrooms, family rooms and stairs. It's not recommended for kitchens, bathrooms or basements that could get damp.
