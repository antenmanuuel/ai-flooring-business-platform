# Choosing flooring for each room

## Kitchens
Kitchens need flooring that handles spills, dropped items and lots of standing. Vinyl plank and porcelain tile are the most practical choices; engineered wood works with care around the sink and dishwasher.

## Bathrooms and laundry rooms
Choose waterproof flooring: porcelain or ceramic tile, or vinyl plank. Avoid solid hardwood and carpet.

## Basements
Basements can be damp, so vinyl plank or tile are the safest choices. Solid hardwood isn't recommended below ground.

## Living rooms, bedrooms and hallways
Hardwood and engineered wood add warmth and value. Laminate gives a similar look at a lower price and resists scratches. Carpet is a comfortable option for bedrooms.

## Homes with pets or kids
Vinyl plank and laminate resist scratches and spills best. If you prefer real wood, choose a harder species and a lower-gloss finish, which hides scratches better.

## Installation patterns
Straight layouts are the most economical. Diagonal, herringbone and chevron patterns look striking but need extra cuts, which takes more time and more material for waste.
