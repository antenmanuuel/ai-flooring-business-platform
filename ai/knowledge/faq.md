# Frequently asked questions

## Where do you work?
Anna Flooring supplies and installs flooring across Metro Detroit. For an address outside the area, call (313) 555-0142 to check.

## What flooring do you install?
Hardwood, engineered wood, vinyl plank, porcelain and ceramic tile, laminate and carpet. The products and prices currently offered are listed in the catalog on the home page.

## Are the catalog prices the final price?
No. Catalog prices are material prices before installation. Your final price comes from Anna's itemized quote, which includes labor, removal of old flooring if needed, and extra material for waste and cuts.

## Why do I need an account, and why must it be approved?
An account lets you send quote requests and follow your projects on your dashboard. New accounts are approved by Anna before they can log in, to keep requests from real customers.

## Can I change or cancel a request?
Call (313) 555-0142 and Anna will help.

## How do I contact Anna Flooring?
Call (313) 555-0142, or send a quote request through your dashboard.
