# Caring for hardwood and engineered wood floors

## Everyday cleaning
Sweep or vacuum (with the beater bar off) a few times a week to remove grit, which acts like sandpaper underfoot. Clean with a damp, well-wrung mop and a cleaner made for wood floors. Never wet-mop or steam-clean wood, and avoid oil soaps, wax and vinegar, which can dull or damage the finish.

## Preventing damage
Put mats at entrances and felt pads under furniture. Wipe up spills right away. Keep pets' nails trimmed. Use rugs in high-traffic areas, and close blinds where strong sun would fade the wood over time.

## Humidity
Wood expands and contracts with humidity. Keeping indoor humidity roughly between 35 and 55 percent all year helps prevent gaps in winter and cupping in summer.

## Refinishing
Solid hardwood can be sanded and refinished several times over its life. Engineered wood can be refinished fewer times, depending on the thickness of its top wood layer. Light wear can often be fixed with a screen-and-recoat instead of a full refinish.
