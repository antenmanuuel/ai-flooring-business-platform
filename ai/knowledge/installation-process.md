# The installation process

## Before installation day
Clear the rooms of furniture, rugs and anything fragile, or let us know ahead of time if you need help moving large items. Solid hardwood and some engineered products need to acclimate in the home for a few days before installation so they adjust to its temperature and humidity. Keep the heating or air conditioning running normally during that time.

## Removing the old floor
If you asked for existing flooring to be removed, it's taken up and hauled away first. The subfloor is then checked: it needs to be clean, dry, flat and solid. Low spots, squeaks or moisture problems are fixed before the new floor goes down, because they affect how long it lasts.

## On the day
Most single rooms take one day; larger or multi-room projects take longer, and patterns such as herringbone or chevron take more time than straight layouts. Expect some noise and dust from cutting. Trim and transitions between rooms are fitted at the end.

## After installation
Wait at least 24 hours before moving heavy furniture back, and use felt pads under furniture legs. Glue-down and tile floors may need longer before full use; you'll be told how long for your floor. Keep leftover boxes or tiles for future repairs.
