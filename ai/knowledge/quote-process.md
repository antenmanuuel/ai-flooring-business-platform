# Getting a quote

## How to request a quote
Create a client account on the website, then wait for Anna to approve it; new accounts can't log in until they're approved. Once you're logged in, open your dashboard and choose "Request a quote". The form asks for the service address, an optional preferred installation date and budget, and each room you want floored.

## Describing your rooms
For every room, give it a name (for example Kitchen or Basement), choose a flooring product from the catalog, and enter either the length and width in feet or the total area in square feet. You can also note the current flooring, whether it should be removed, and the installation pattern: straight, diagonal, herringbone or chevron. You can add up to 5 photos (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each), which help Anna see the space and the existing floor.

## The estimate on the form
While you fill in the form, it shows a materials estimate based on the catalog price and the area. It covers materials only, before labor, removal of old flooring, extra material for waste and cuts, and any other charges. Products sold per piece or per roll are priced in the quote instead.

## What happens next
Each request gets a number such as R-1002. Anna reviews it and sends an itemized quote, which appears on your dashboard. Your project's page shows its status, from "Under review" to "Quote ready". If anything is unclear, she may contact you by phone or email.
