# Caring for porcelain and ceramic tile

## Cleaning tile
Sweep or vacuum, then mop with warm water and a pH-neutral cleaner. Porcelain is dense and very water-resistant, which suits bathrooms, entryways and mudrooms. Avoid harsh acids and bleach on colored grout.

## Grout
Grout is the part that stains. Sealing cement-based grout after installation and every year or two afterwards makes it much easier to keep clean. A soft brush and a grout cleaner handle most stains.

## Comfort and durability
Tile is very durable but hard and cool underfoot; rugs help in living areas. A dropped heavy object can crack a tile, so keep a few spare tiles from the installation for repairs.
