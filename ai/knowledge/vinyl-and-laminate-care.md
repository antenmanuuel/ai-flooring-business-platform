# Caring for vinyl plank and laminate floors

## Vinyl plank
Luxury vinyl plank is waterproof, which makes it a good choice for kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms and basements. Sweep or vacuum regularly and mop with a pH-neutral cleaner. Avoid rubber-backed mats, which can stain vinyl, and protect it from dragging heavy furniture.

## Laminate
Laminate has a hard, scratch-resistant wear layer that handles pets and busy households well. Many laminates resist water on the surface, but standing water can still seep into the seams, so wipe spills quickly and mop with a barely damp mop. Never steam-clean laminate.

## Scratches and pets
Both vinyl and laminate resist everyday scratches better than most hardwood. Trimmed pet nails, felt pads and entry mats keep them looking new longer.
